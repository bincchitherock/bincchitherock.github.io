Hi, thank you for taking the time to visit my portfolio. My name is Seo Bin Han (pronounced 'Sobin'). 

I'm currently a senior in Electrical Engineering at UC Davis, with research interests in hardware–algorithm co-design for medical interfaces. In particular, I'm interested in ultra-low-power analog-to-digital conversion, high-ratio lossless data compression, and specialized DSP/ASIC architectures for real-time brain-computer interfaces and other implantable medical systems.

In my free time, I'm usually on an impromptu trip or doing something creative at home. I'm reachable at seohan[at]ucdavis[dot]edu, and am always open to receiving emails •⩊•
